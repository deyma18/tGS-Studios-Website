About

Built to give serious work the infrastructure it deserves

tGS Creative Works is a strategy, management, and media production consultancy. We work across the UK, West Africa, and the United States with people and projects that have substance behind them and need the structure to match.

The Organisation

What tGS Creative Works is.

tGS Creative Works is the consulting and media arm of tGS Studios. It handles the strategy, management, production, and content work that serious projects require before they can move properly in public.

The work spans literary campaigns, training programmes, brand architecture, editorial strategy, media production, and the infrastructure that connects expertise to the right audience. It is not category-specific. The thinking is consistent across all of it.

The Principle

Why the business exists.

The people with the most substantial work behind them are often the least well-positioned in public. Not because the work is weak, but because the infrastructure around it has never been built properly. tGS Creative exists to close that gap.

We start with what is already there: the work, the credibility, the knowledge, and the track record. Then we build the public-facing structure that reflects it accurately and moves it forward with purpose.

Leadership

The person behind the work

Jeleel A. Deyma Ayoade

Director, tGS Creative Works

Deyma leads tGS Creative Works with a background that crosses strategy, publishing, media, and brand development. The consultancy's approach, grounded in market thinking, clear positioning, and disciplined execution, reflects his direct experience of building infrastructure around people and ideas that deserve a wider reach.

He directs the MPS Beauty Marketing Cohort from the UK, manages the literary and media campaign for Adeniyi A. Adebisi, and oversees the development of WYA as a fashion and culture brand. The work is always practical and always specific: no abstract strategies that cannot be executed, no visibility without a structure to support it.

Operating Philosophy

Three things that are always true about how we work.

  • Intelligence before action. We research, map, and understand before we recommend or execute. Decisions are made with the full picture in view.
  • Specificity over generality. Generic strategies produce generic results. Every engagement is built around the particular work, the particular audience, and the particular moment.
  • Execution is the proof. We are not in the business of producing plans that sit in folders. The work is not finished until it is in motion and producing a result.

Where We Operate

UK, West Africa, and the United States.

tGS Creative Works is based in Salford, England. Active engagements are currently running across the UK, Nigeria, and the United States, with the capacity to work in any context where strategy, management, or media production is needed.

The work is not geography-dependent. The thinking travels.

Working With Us

A small number of engagements. Serious attention.

tGS Creative Works does not take on a large volume of clients at any one time. This is deliberate. The quality of the thinking and the depth of the engagement matter more than the quantity of the work.

If you have a project that needs this kind of attention, the best first step is a direct conversation about the work, the goal, and what needs to change.
